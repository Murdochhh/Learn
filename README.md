The CTFs Branch

Every weekend I will attempt one CTF, or CTF like challenge and record it here.
I will record what I was trying to do, and the steps I took to get there.
I will state where I got lost, and what I had to learn to find my way out.
Even if I fail a CTF I will still post it, and I will say where I got lost, and what I tried to do to get out.
